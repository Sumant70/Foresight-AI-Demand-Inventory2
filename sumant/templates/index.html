Validation Protocol (No Lookahead Bias)
Models are trained on historical sales from 2024-01-01 to 2025-11-01 (671 days) and validated against the final 60 days (2025-11-02 to 2025-12-31). No random splitting is used, preserving chronological integrity.

WAPE (Primary Metric)
WAPE = ∑|Actual - Forecast| / ∑Actual × 100
Weights errors by physical sales volume; resilient against zero-demand days where standard MAPE breaks down.
MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
MAE = (1/n) ∑|Actual - Forecast|
Average magnitude of errors expressed directly in daily physical units sold.
RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
RMSE = √[ (1/n) ∑(Actual - Forecast)² ]
Quadratic penalty penalizing large isolated forecast mistakes.
